TASK: EXTRACT FACTS{chunk_note}

From the TRANSCRIPT above only, list the facts that a correct and complete clinical note of this
consultation must contain. Work only from the transcript; never import outside knowledge as if it
were said. A garbled, ambiguous or contradicted turn never becomes a confident fact.

Cover: the presenting complaint; key history; significant positive findings; pertinent negatives
the patient denied or the clinician noted as absent; examination findings (or that none was done
in a remote consultation); the working diagnosis as stated, keeping any hedging; every element of
the plan, with drug names, doses, frequencies and durations as said; safety-netting; material
social or risk history.

For each fact give:
- "fact": one atomic fact, precisely stated, keeping hedging, conditions and negation as spoken.
- "category": one of presenting_complaint, symptom, history, family_social, medicine, allergy,
  examination, investigation, assessment, plan, follow_up.
- "attributes": any of negated, family, hypothetical, historical that apply (else empty).
- "evidence": a short verbatim quote from ONE turn of the transcript (at most 25 words) that
  supports the fact.

Leave out conversational closure, identity checks, reassurance boilerplate and generic patient
education unless clinically load-bearing for this consultation.
