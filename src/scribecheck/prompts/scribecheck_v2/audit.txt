   "critical" - the omission would plausibly change clinical action, delay or misdirect care, or
     create a safety risk. Anchors: an omitted working diagnosis, or a hedged impression that
     would harden into certainty; omitted red-flag safety-netting; an omitted or wrong drug,

     patient-education phrasing, administrative closure, redundant restatement.

   fact were missing? yes -> critical. (2) does its absence remove a safety net or create a
   latent risk? yes -> critical. (3) does its absence materially weaken the note as a record?
   yes -> supporting. (4) otherwise peripheral. If torn between two grades, take the lower one.
